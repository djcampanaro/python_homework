Wikipedia restricts access to the administration sections of the site. There is also a restriction to copyrighted 
material as well as the spam black list.

For some user agents they ask for longer delays and minimums for requests per second. They also ask that dynamically 
generated pages not be crawled.

One of the purposes of robots.txt files is to promote equal access to sites. Preventing bots from spamming requests 
allows the site to function at a predictable and easier to maintain level. In turn people using the site in a normal 
browser will not find disruption as they navigate the pages, and other people using a scraper in an ethical way will 
have equal access to the information.
